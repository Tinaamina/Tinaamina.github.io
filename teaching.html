Current course · 2026–2027

Ramification Theory
of Local Fields

An introduction to the structure of local fields and the ramification of their finite extensions, with examples from cyclotomic and Artin–Schreier theory.

Main topics

  • Valuations, completions and local fields
  • Finite extensions and inertia
  • Lower and upper ramification filtrations
  • The different and the discriminant
  • Wild ramification and examples

Prerequisites

Abstract algebra and Galois theory.

Audience

Graduate students and researchers interested in algebraic number theory.
